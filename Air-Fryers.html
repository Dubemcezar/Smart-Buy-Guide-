Best Air Fryers for Everyday Cooking

Air fryers have become a popular kitchen appliance because they can make crispy meals with considerably less oil than traditional deep frying. But with so many models available, choosing the right one can be confusing.

This guide explains what to look for before buying an air fryer and how to choose one that fits your cooking needs.

What Should You Look For in an Air Fryer?

1. Capacity

Consider how many people you normally cook for. A smaller air fryer may work well for one or two people, while larger households may benefit from a bigger basket or dual-basket design.

2. Temperature Range

A useful air fryer should provide a flexible temperature range so you can prepare different foods such as chicken, vegetables, fries and baked foods.

3. Ease of Cleaning

Removable baskets and dishwasher-safe components can make cleaning much easier after cooking.

4. Cooking Presets

Preset cooking programs can be convenient for beginners, especially if you don't want to manually calculate cooking temperatures and times.

5. Countertop Space

Check the dimensions before buying. A large air fryer may offer more cooking capacity but can take up significant countertop space.

Air Fryer Types to Consider

Basket Air Fryers

These are compact and straightforward. They are a popular choice for everyday meals and smaller kitchens.

Dual-Basket Air Fryers

Dual-basket models allow you to cook two different foods at the same time, which can be useful when preparing complete meals.

Air Fryer Ovens

These combine air-frying functionality with a larger oven-style cooking space and may offer additional cooking modes.

Quick Buying Checklist

  • Choose a capacity that matches your household.
  • Check the temperature range.
  • Look for easy-to-clean components.
  • Compare cooking presets and functions.
  • Check the appliance dimensions.
  • Read recent customer reviews before buying.
  • Compare prices from reliable retailers.
Smart Buy Tip: Don't automatically choose the biggest or most expensive air fryer. The best option is the one whose capacity and features actually match how you cook.
