Desk Setup Essentials

A productive desk does not need to be expensive. Prioritize the items that improve comfort, organization and daily workflow.

What to consider

  • Lighting: A good desk lamp can reduce eye strain and make late-night work easier.
  • Ergonomics: Choose a comfortable chair and position your screen at a sensible height.
  • Organization: Cable management and a small desk organizer can remove a surprising amount of clutter.
  • Input devices: A comfortable keyboard and mouse matter if you spend hours coding or studying.
  • Power: Use quality chargers and power accessories from reputable manufacturers.

Note: Product prices, specifications and availability can change. Check the retailer's current information before purchasing.
