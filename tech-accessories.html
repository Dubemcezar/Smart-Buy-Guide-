BUYING GUIDE

Everyday Tech Accessories

Small accessories can save time and frustration, but quality matters. Use this checklist before buying.

What to consider

  • Compatibility: Check your device ports, charging standards and supported wattage.
  • Durability: Look for reinforced connectors and credible warranty information.
  • Safety: Avoid suspiciously cheap chargers and batteries with unclear specifications.
  • Portability: Choose compact accessories if you travel or work from multiple locations.
  • Value: Compare the total usefulness of a product rather than buying based on the lowest price.

Note: Product prices, specifications and availability can change. Check the retailer's current information before purchasing.
