How to Choose Budget Headphones

Affordable headphones can be good enough for music, study and calls if you focus on the right features rather than the biggest brand name.

What to consider

  • Comfort: Look for a fit you can wear for long study or travel sessions.
  • Battery life: For wireless models, compare claimed playback time and charging convenience.
  • Microphone: If you take calls, check independent microphone tests where available.
  • Connection: Bluetooth reliability matters more than a long list of unused features.
  • Reviews: Look for repeated comments about durability, not one-off complaints or praise.

Note: Product prices, specifications and availability can change. Check the retailer's current information before purchasing.
